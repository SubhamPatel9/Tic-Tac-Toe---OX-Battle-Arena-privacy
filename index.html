Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Toe: OX Battle Arena

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Summary: Tic Tac Toe: OX Battle Arena is built with an offline-first architecture. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal information, contacts, passwords, or device locations.

1. Overview & Information We Do Not Collect

This privacy policy applies to the mobile application Tic Tac Toe: OX Battle Arena (Package ID: com.orinexa.tictactoeoxbattlearena.zcpzsj).

We respect your privacy and believe in complete transparency. Our game does not require account creation, sign-in, or registration.

  • No Personal Data: We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, address, or demographic data.
  • No Device Access: We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photo library, or precise GPS location.
  • No Cloud Sync Required: Gameplay, level progress, coins, and unlocks operate fully on your local device.

2. Local Data Storage & Persistence

The game stores local configuration files and a local SQLite database directly on your device using Android Room. This data includes:

  • Player level, earned XP, and coin balance.
  • Single-player campaign puzzle stars and completed levels (1–100).
  • Boss Rush defeat records and high scores.
  • Unlocked cosmetic skins (X skins, O skins, avatars, and boards).
  • In-game audio, haptic, and animation preferences.

This data remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded to any external server. You have complete control to wipe this data at any time by selecting Reset Progress in the Settings menu or clearing app data in your device settings.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

When your device is connected to the internet, the game may use Google AdMob (Google Play Services) to display banner, interstitial, or optional rewarded video ads for in-game cosmetic rewards.

Google AdMob may collect and process pseudonymous identifiers and device data to serve ads in compliance with Google Play Developer Program policies and global data privacy regulations (such as GDPR and CCPA):

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID).
  • IP address (for coarse geographic ad delivery and fraud prevention).
  • Device model, operating system version, and screen metrics.
  • Ad interaction events (impressions, clicks, completed video views).

For more details on how Google handles advertising data, please review:

Google Advertising & Privacy Policy

4. Children’s Privacy

Our game is designed for all audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age in your jurisdiction).

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information through a third-party SDK, please contact us so that we can take appropriate measures.

5. Device Permissions

The app requests only standard Android permissions necessary for gameplay features:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Provides tactile haptic feedback during piece placement and victory effects (can be toggled in Settings).
  • android.permission.INTERNET & android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Allows loading optional rewarded ads and checking network availability.

6. Security

Because your progress data resides exclusively on your local device, its security is governed by Android's built-in application sandboxing. We do not maintain any central servers or external databases containing user records.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically for any changes.
